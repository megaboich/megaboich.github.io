Résumé: Oleksandr Boiko

Software engineer with 20+ years of experience in building enterprise web applications, mostly on the frontend side. Most of my career I worked on data-heavy UIs: reporting, analytics, dashboards and graphs.

Besides product features, I like building things that help the team work faster: tools for developing UI components, test automation, type checking, and lately workflows with AI coding agents. I prefer simple and well supported solutions which are easy to maintain, and I like sharing what I learn with colleagues.

Experience

    Frontend technical lead of the Process Mining product, working on its UI together with the team. My favorite parts are analytics, comparison of process models, and especially the interactive process graph, where I research layout improvements with different algorithms and approaches. Before that worked on a platform-wide filters framework for dashboards and on Performance Analytics.
